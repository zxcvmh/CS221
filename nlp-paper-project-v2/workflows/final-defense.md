# Workflow: Final Defense

Goal: convert evidence and results into a defensible scientific narrative.

Sequence:
`EVIDENCE → RESULTS → ERROR ANALYSIS → CLAIMS → LIMITATIONS → DEFENSE QUESTIONS`

Final claim record:
```yaml
claim:
evidence:
scope:
confidence:
alternative_explanation:
limitation:
```

Suggested presentation:
1. application/motivation
2. VQA failure mode
3. NLP research question
4. main ACL paper
5. mechanism
6. reproducibility evidence
7. research gap
8. controlled intervention
9. quantitative results
10. stratified/error analysis
11. limitations
12. conclusion/Q&A

Never say “our method is better” without metric, dataset, comparison, controls, uncertainty, and scope.

G5 reviewer audit is required before presentation.
